## The problem

Have a bot that is able to generate easy set up, no screen activities to entertain kids (pick age) for up to 1hr. Either indoor / outdoor.

## Why it matters

We live in an "always on" world, and kids have caught it. "I'm bored" lands about four minutes after you've sat down, attention spans are tiny, and everything is a request for entertainment right now.

Meanwhile, mum needs an hour. Just one. To do the admin that's been sitting there for a week, hide in a cupboard with a book, or honestly just sleep.

The easy fix is a screen, but most of us would rather not reach for it every time. So we go looking for alternatives and find Pinterest boards that need a glue gun and 45 minutes of prep, or "ideas" that keep the kids busy for six minutes flat. We've already tried the Sleeping Beauty game (lol), and it works exactly once.

What's missing is something that gives us that hour: easy to set up, made from stuff we already have, and fun enough that the kids forget we're gone.

## Pitch

**Give me that hour**

"I'm bored" arrives roughly four minutes after you sit down.

Kids today are wired for constant entertainment. Attention spans are short, and you are the entertainment.

You don't need a day off. You need one hour: for the admin, the book, or the nap in the cupboard. (Yes, the cupboard. No, they haven't found you yet.)

You could hand over a screen, and sometimes you will. But you've also tried the Pinterest activities that need a glue gun and a degree. You've played the Sleeping Beauty game. It worked once. It was beautiful. It will not happen again.

So here's the idea: ten minutes of setup, with things you already own, and the kids stay busy while you get your hour back.

Because the cupboard is getting crowded.
